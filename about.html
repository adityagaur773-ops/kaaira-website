Our Story

Every Mirror Begins With A Story

KAIRA was born from a passion for Indian craftsmanship and a desire to create luxury mirrors that tell a story. Every piece is handcrafted by skilled artisans who pour their expertise into each design.

From selecting premium upholstery fabrics to the final polish, every step is done with meticulous attention to detail. We believe in creating pieces that transform spaces and become heirlooms.
